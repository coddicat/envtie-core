# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community. Be considerate, discuss technical disagreements in good
faith, respect privacy, and do not publish another person’s credentials or customer data.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, and publishing private
information without permission. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and restrict participation when
needed to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to `coddicat@gmail.com`. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
